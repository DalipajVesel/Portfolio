# AI in Education

A systematic literature review on generative AI chatbots and how universities
use them, done as a group of five with the Arksey and O'Malley scoping review
method. The paper cites 20 publications from Google Scholar and the ACM
Digital Library.

Technical Writing course, spring 2025-26.

The documents are the deliverable: the paper, the stage documents, the
literature matrix with the 20 papers, the Gantt chart and the presentation.
